This Week's Reflection: Life, Work, and God's Blessings

I hope you enjoyed the beautiful, crisp fall weather this week! I went running several times just to enjoy the colorful trees, crystal-clear blue skies, and refreshing autumn breeze. What a wonderful time of year! I hope you also get a chance to enjoy this beautiful season before all the leaves fall to the ground.

During my runs, while listening to 93.3 FM and reflecting on Bible verses, I found myself thinking about how much I have to be thankful for. One thing that especially came to mind was how incredibly blessed I am to have a job that I truly love.

I love being a professor! I love the freedom and flexibility that come with my job. I love interacting with students and being surrounded by young people who are experiencing one of the most exciting stages of their lives. Their smiles, energy, enthusiasm, and even their occasional immaturity, strong egos, and stubborn opinions make my days interesting. Haha! Despite all that, they are generally kind, sweet, warm, and respectful toward me. Being around them keeps me feeling young, too!

I also love having my own office, where I can sit quietly, think, read, and spend time researching topics that genuinely interest me. What makes it even more rewarding is knowing that other scholars in my field find my research valuable. Over my 21-year academic career, my research has been cited more than 10,000 times—that's an average of nearly 500 citations per year! This has contributed to my recognition among the world's top 2% of scientists. I must admit, this makes me feel very proud of what I have accomplished over the years.

I am also fortunate to work at a highly ranked business school. I have to confess that I sometimes feel a little boastful when telling others where I work. Haha!

And there are so many other things to appreciate. My salary is neither too high nor too low, but it is more than enough to provide a comfortable life for Mom and me and help support you girls. I enjoy several months of breaks from teaching each year, which I consider an incredible luxury. Even during the academic year, I have a great deal of control over my daily schedule—when to come to the office, what to work on, and how to manage my time. Not many professions offer this kind of freedom.

The more I think about all these things, the more I realize how generously God has blessed me with a profession that fits my interests, personality, and lifestyle. I am deeply grateful to Him for leading me to this career. I hope to continue doing this work for as long as my health and passion allow.

And this brings me to something I sincerely wish for both of you. I pray that each of you will eventually find a career that you genuinely love—a job that gives you satisfaction, a sense of purpose, and pride in what you do, while also filling your heart with gratitude and humility toward God.

There is no need to rush. Take your time, explore your interests, and keep praying that God will guide you toward the right path. When you discover something that truly excites you and feels meaningful, pursue it wholeheartedly.

Of course, whatever career you choose, reaching that goal may require considerable effort, additional education or training, and sacrifices of time and money. But I believe those sacrifices can be well worth it when they lead you to something you truly love.

Just look at my own experience. It took me about 11 years to establish myself in this profession—five years to earn my Ph.D., during which I survived on a very small paycheck, followed by another six years of hard work, pressure, and many sleepless nights to earn tenure. It certainly wasn't an easy journey! But looking back, I can confidently say that it was worth every sacrifice.

I don't know exactly what God has planned for your careers or where He will lead you. But I sincerely hope and pray that, someday, you will look back on your own journeys and say, just as I do today, "I love my job, I am happy with what I do, and I am so thankful to God for leading me here."

That would make me a very happy dad.

Enjoy this beautiful fall season, and always remember how much I love you both!

Love,

Your blessed and grateful Dad ❤️
